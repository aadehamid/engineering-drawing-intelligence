# DEC-054: The owner's delegation takes precedence over an earlier reservation, so W-SP's contents stand

Status: Decided
Date: 2026-10-13

## Context

[DEC-036](DEC-036-w-sp-new-plant.md) left W-SP's exact contents to come to the
owner as a pull request. During the night run, the agent chose them under the
owner's delegation in [DEC-048](DEC-048-night-delegation.md), and recorded them
as [DEC-052](DEC-052-w-sp-contents.md). The handoff of 2026-10-13 asked the owner
to review them, because DEC-036 had reserved them for the owner.

## Decision

When a delegation and an earlier reservation for the owner cover the same
choice, the delegation takes precedence. DEC-052 stands as decided, and W-SP's
contents need no further owner review.

## Alternatives considered

- The owner reviews W-SP's contents before work builds on them. That is what
  DEC-036's wording asked for, and the owner chose not to.

## Owner's words

Asked as the second of three questions after the handoff of 2026-10-13: review
W-SP's contents, which DEC-052 made under delegation while DEC-036 reserved them
for the owner.

The owner answered:

> My delegation takes precedence. You can settel it

## Evidence

- DEC-048 records the delegation, and DEC-052 the contents chosen under it.
